# Numerical Optimization

Projects that study optimization algorithms through explicit implementations and applied examples.

## Projects

- [`garch_11_mle`](garch_11_mle/): Gaussian conditional maximum-likelihood estimation of a GARCH(1,1) model with an analytic score, a from-scratch BFGS optimizer, statistical inference and reference-solver validation.
- [`primal_dual_svm`](primal_dual_svm/): primal subgradient and dual quadratic-program optimization for a soft-margin SVM on the Wisconsin breast-cancer data.
- [`matrix_factorization_recommender`](matrix_factorization_recommender/): alternating least squares and SGD for a MovieLens recommender objective.
- [`logistic_regression`](logistic_regression/): gradient/Newton optimization of penalized logistic likelihood on real clinical data.
- [`lasso_elastic_net`](lasso_elastic_net/): proximal-gradient and coordinate-descent regularization paths on the diabetes regression data.
- [`ridge_regression_pokemon`](ridge_regression_pokemon/): comparison of optimization methods for Ridge Regression using Pokémon attributes.
